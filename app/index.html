Get a second look at 5 common findings and a referral suggestion. It runs on this device and nothing is uploaded.

  1. Frontal view only (PA or AP). Lateral films aren't supported.
  2. Use an image file, or photograph the film on a lightbox, filling the frame.
  3. Read the result as a prompt to look again, not as a diagnosis.

    About ChexLite

    A MobileNetV3 classifier (~17 MB) trained on CheXpert, running offline in your browser.

    • The referral threshold errs toward referring: it was set to catch 95% of studies with any of the 5 findings on the development data, and caught 92% on the radiologist-read test.
    • Trained on films from one US hospital. Not tested on phone photos of films, children or other populations; expect lower accuracy there.
    • R and L markers assume a standard frontal film (the patient's right on the image's left).
